## Frozen Thesis

On previously unseen compositional commercial instructions, ECOCOMMIT must achieve high semantic reliability at useful autonomous coverage and ultimately reduce pre-registered Total Economic Loss relative to the strongest dynamic deterministic workflow while maintaining acceptable legitimate-transaction completion and latency.

## Engineering Principle

Probabilistic intent interpretation is treated as untrusted. Financial guarantees begin only after economically material intent is represented in a provenance-preserving contract and unresolved material ambiguity is surfaced rather than guessed.
