# Frozen MVP Scope

## Included
- Autonomous enterprise procurement only.
- Natural-language procurement mandates.
- Economic contract schema.
- Intent interpretation provider interface.
- Provenance-preserving contract generation.
- Fidelity, structural, ambiguity and abstention checks.
- Later milestones: fixed policy classes, controlled authoritative evidence, deterministic exposure policy, Razorpay Test Mode.

## Excluded from Checkpoint A
- Universal commerce ontology.
- Consumer shopping assistant.
- Real supplier integrations.
- Production payment execution.
- Autonomous compensation.
- Multi-agent orchestration.
